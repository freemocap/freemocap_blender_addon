# Skelly FreeMoCap

Skelly FreeMoCap is provided under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Credit to the **FreeMoCap Foundation** is appreciated but not required. We also
ask that Skelly-related assets be made available **free of charge to end users**.
These are voluntary requests, not license conditions; commercial use,
modification, and redistribution remain permitted.

The machine-readable [asset definition](skelly_asset.json) records the grant,
requests and scope: the existing `skelly_full_mesh_20k_faces.blend` artwork,
its `skelly_mesh.json.gz` representation, and generated Skelly VRM avatars.
The application and exporter software license is unchanged.

VRM1 files retain the format's required VRM Public License metadata. Skelly
FreeMoCap is also separately offered under CC0 as stated here; the additional
license link is not a claim that it overrides the VRM format license.
